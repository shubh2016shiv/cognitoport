Healthcare Business Problem- AI Agent Workflow for filtering preferred prescription drugs from a 5,000+ drug database

In many e-prescribing systems, when a doctor prescribes a drug, the system shows every drug covered by the patient’s insurance, sometimes leaving the doctor with dozens of options even though only a few may be clinically preferred.

The challenge is that these two sources use different names. A clinical recommendation might say “ADDERALL tablet 15 mg”, while the insurance data stores the same drug as “Amphetamine-Dextroamphetamine”.

This pipeline bridges that gap by understanding drug names, active ingredients, combination drugs, drug classes, and different strengths and forms, then identifying which of the patient’s covered drugs are also clinically recommended. It does this across seven insurance plans, with a hard safety rule: a drug can only be marked as preferred if it is already covered by the patient’s insurance. The result is a smaller, clinically relevant set of choices for the doctor without ever adding a drug that the insurance does not cover.
